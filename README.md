# Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, PhD

**Geneticist | Cancer Genomics | Functional & Regulatory Genomics | Bioinformatics | AI-assisted Scientific Research**

I am a PhD geneticist and cancer genomics researcher working at the intersection of **molecular genetics, functional genomics, genomic data analysis, and translational biotechnology**.

My research background includes CRISPR/Cas9-mediated functional studies, regulatory genomics, cancer biology, NGS/WES analysis, variant interpretation, and reproducible computational workflows. I also use AI-assisted tools to support scientific analysis, research workflows, and reproducible documentation.
