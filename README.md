# Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, Ph.D.

**Geneticist | Cancer & Functional Genomics | Molecular Diagnostics | Computational Genomics | Laboratory Quality**

**Location:** Iran

Ph.D. geneticist with professional experience in molecular and cytogenetic diagnostics, cancer functional genomics, laboratory supervision, and scientific education. My doctoral research investigated a HER2-associated regulatory enhancer in breast cancer using CRISPR/Cas9, integrating experimental molecular genetics with bioinformatic interpretation.

My computational portfolio contains independent, project-based workflows and case studies in WES, viral genomics, RNA-seq, variant interpretation, and molecular-dynamics simulation. These activities are distinguished from my professional diagnostic-laboratory experience.

## Selected research outputs

### HER2 enhancer functional genomics
Doctoral research using CRISPR/Cas9 to investigate a HER2-associated enhancer in breast cancer cell lines. Related findings have been published in peer-reviewed journals.

- [Research repository](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/HER2-Enhancer-Functional-Analysis)
- [Scientific Reports publication](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-46460-x)

### PX459-v2 gRNA cloning under resource constraints
Retrospective research artifact documenting a resource-constrained workflow adaptation. Conclusions are limited to feasibility under the reported constraints; superiority or quantitative equivalence is not claimed.

- [Repository](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/px459-v2-resource-constrained-cloning)
- [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22086082)

### Manual FFPE DNA extraction for long-amplicon PCR
Retrospective workflow documentation reporting recovery of longer PCR-amplifiable DNA fragments in a subset of samples, without claiming universal superiority.

- [Repository](https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/ffpe-manual-dna-extraction-long-amplicon-pcr)
- [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22091030)
