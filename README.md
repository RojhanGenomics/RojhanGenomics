# Mahdieh Rojhannezhad, Ph.D.

**Genetics & Biomedical Researcher | Functional & Regulatory Genomics | Cancer Genomics | CRISPR/Cas9 | Bioinformatics**

I am a Ph.D. geneticist and biomedical researcher working at the intersection of **functional and regulatory genomics, cancer biology, molecular genetics, genomic data analysis, and reproducible research**.

My research background combines experimental genetics with computational analysis, including CRISPR/Cas9-based functional studies, regulatory enhancer characterization, molecular diagnostics, NGS/WES analysis, variant interpretation, and reproducible workflow development. I also use AI-assisted tools to support scientific analysis, research documentation, evaluation, and reproducibility.

## 🔬 Featured Research Artifacts

These two DOI-archived projects represent my current focus on **researcher-led protocol optimization, evidence-aware documentation, and reproducible scientific artifacts**.

### 1. PX459-v2 gRNA Cloning Under Resource Constraints

A retrospective research artifact documenting a resource-constrained adaptation of a PX459-v2 gRNA cloning workflow. The project distinguishes documented observations from reconstructed or unsupported details and explicitly limits its conclusions to **feasibility under the reported constraints**, without claiming protocol superiority.

- **GitHub:** https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/px459-v2-resource-constrained-cloning
- **Zenodo DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22086082
- **Version:** v1.0.1

### 2. Manual FFPE DNA Extraction for Long-Amplicon PCR

A retrospective research artifact documenting optimization of a manual FFPE DNA-extraction workflow for recovery of longer PCR-amplifiable DNA fragments under resource-constrained laboratory conditions. The project reports its outcome boundaries explicitly and does not claim universal superiority over commercial extraction methods.

- **GitHub:** https://github.com/RojhanGenomics/ffpe-manual-dna-extraction-long-amplicon-pcr
- **Zenodo DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22091030
- **Version:** v1.0.1

### Research background

**HER2 regulatory enhancer functional genomics**  
Doctoral research investigating a HER2-associated regulatory enhancer in breast cancer using CRISPR/Cas9, integrating experimental molecular genetics with bioinformatic interpretation. This work resulted in peer-reviewed publications including *Scientific Reports*.

**Clinical molecular and cytogenetic genetics**  
More than five years of experience across molecular and cytogenetic diagnostic laboratories, including laboratory supervision, workflow coordination, quality-oriented practice, and protocol optimization.

**Reproducible computational genomics**  
Independent development of reproducible NGS workflows using Nextflow DSL2, including WES and viral-genomics analysis, with containerized execution and documented computational workflows.

**Structural and computational biology**  
Current development of molecular-dynamics workflows for cancer-associated protein systems using GROMACS and related structural-bioinformatics tools.
